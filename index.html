How SafeVision works

  1. Look. Your camera frames are analysed by COCO-SSD, a pre-trained model running through TensorFlow.js.
  2. Judge. The larger an object appears in the frame, the closer it probably is. Big, confident detections of hazard objects trigger an alert.
  3. Warn. A red overlay marks the hazard, a banner names it, and optional voice, beep or vibration gets your attention.

Privacy

Video stays in your browser. Only the model files are downloaded, once, when you first start.

Limits

Distance is estimated from object size, not measured. Speed, direction and lighting are not considered. Expect false alarms and misses.

Not a safety device

This is a prototype. Never rely on it for driving, cycling or walking. Stay aware of your surroundings.
